Editorial: one chart, one finding. Write the title as the finding
("出口连续第三个季度下滑", "Exports fell for the third quarter"), with units
and period in a muted subtitle and a one-line source or note in `.text-small`
under the chart. Colour the one series the sentence is about with
`--viz-series-1`; draw the rest in `--viz-context` (`theme.context`) and label
every line directly at its end instead of a legend. Value-axis gridlines only,
no value-axis line, points hidden except a dot on the last value of the
highlighted series. Annotate the moment that matters with a short clause and
a thin leader line — at most two callouts. No gradient fills, no rounded bars,
no decorative icons. Headlines take `--font-display`; ticks, labels and
numbers stay in `--font-sans`.
